A new town NPC who looks at everything in your inventory and nearby chests, and tells you exactly what you can craft — right now.

No menus. No chest-by-chest checking. Just talk to him.

Works with every mod that registers recipes through tModLoader — Calamity and more.

[h3]Arrival[/h3]
He arrives at your world's spawn point the moment the world is created — no boss fight, no rare drop, no condition. Build him a vacant house and he moves in.

[h3]Full Crafting Path[/h3]
The core of this mod. For every craftable item, click once and the entire recipe chain unfolds down to raw materials — recipes of recipes of recipes, all in a single tree. Each material shows how many you have and how many you need, so you know exactly what to gather next.

[i]He answers before you ask.[/i]
