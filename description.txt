A new town NPC who lists every item you can craft right now — recipes of recipes of recipes, traced all the way to raw materials.

Works with every mod that registers recipes through tModLoader — Calamity, Magic Storage, and more.

[h3]Arrival[/h3]
He arrives at your world's spawn point the moment the world is created — no boss fight, no rare drop, no condition. Build him a vacant house and he moves in.

[h3]Full Crafting Path[/h3]
For every craftable item, click once and the entire recipe chain unfolds down to raw materials — recipes of recipes of recipes, all in a single tree. Each material shows how many you have and how many you need, so you know exactly what to gather next.

[i]He answers before you ask.[/i]
