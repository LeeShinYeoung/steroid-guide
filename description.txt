A new town NPC who lists every item you can craft right now — recipes of recipes of recipes, traced all the way to raw materials.

[h3]Arrival[/h3]
He arrives at your world's spawn point the moment the world is created — no boss fight, no rare drop, no condition. Build him a vacant house and he moves in.

[h3]Full Crafting Path[/h3]
The core of this mod. It lists final items that require multiple crafting steps, and clicking one unfolds the entire chain down to raw materials in a tree structure. Each material shows how many you have and how many you need, so you know exactly what to gather.

[h3]Auto Scan[/h3]
Talk to the NPC and he automatically analyzes your inventory and every chest within 60 tiles of you. No need to open chests one by one — he evaluates craftability based on everything you have. The number of chests being referenced is displayed on screen.

[h3]Filter & Search[/h3]
Filter by category — weapons, armor, accessories, tools, consumables, placeables, materials, and more. Sort by rarity or name, and search by item name to quickly find what you're looking for.

[i]He answers before you ask.[/i]
